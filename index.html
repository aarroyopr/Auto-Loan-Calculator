How It Works

Four simple steps to your estimate

No sign-ups, no credit pulls, no commitments. Enter your details and see your payment instantly.

01
💰
Enter the vehicle price
Type the asking price and your down payment. The financed amount and eligible term tier are calculated automatically.
02
📊
Select your FICO tier
Choose your credit score range. Each tier maps directly to our current APR schedule.
03
📅
Pick the vehicle year & term
Maximum term depends on both the vehicle price and model year. Options update automatically.
04
🛡️
Add optional coverages
Toggle GAP insurance or extended warranty to see their effect on your total monthly payment.
